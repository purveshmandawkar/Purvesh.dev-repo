About

Background

Started with curiosity. Saw a circuit work for the first time and realized electronics isn't theory — it's a tool to build real products. That's what pulled me deeper.

Since then: 50+ client builds, real field deployments, and hardware that has to work when it matters — not just on a demo bench. Currently 2nd year ENTC at Sinhgad College of Engineering, Pune. Targeting core embedded systems internship.
